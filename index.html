Dataset & Methodology

Built on real data. Grounded in real roads.

Every score, forecast, and route is reproducible from the provided BTP dataset and freely available OpenStreetMap data.

BTP Dataset

248,231 Validated Violations

Cleaned from 298,450 raw records. Excel-rename-robust ingestion. Features: geo-coordinates, violation type, vehicle type, police station, timestamp. Date range Nov 2023 - Apr 2024.

Spatial Indexing

H3 Hexagon Zones (Resolution 9)

Uber's H3 hexagonal hierarchical indexing groups violations into ~170 m hexagonal cells. Aggregates peak hour, dominant violation type, top station, and total count per zone. Reproducible at any resolution.

hex_id = h3.geo_to_h3(lat, lon, resolution=9)
PICS Score

Parking-Induced Congestion Score

A branded, transparent 0-100 score fusing four factors. Arterials and junction-adjacent zones scored higher than quiet lanes, even at lower violation counts.

PICS = 100 × norm( log1p(
  severity × persistence
  × road_factor × junction_factor
))
Road Network

Real Bengaluru OSM Roads

569,474 OSM road edges + 166,191 junctions downloaded via OSMnx (cached). Each hotspot zone is snapped to nearest real road. Road class weights and lane factors applied for defensible PICS grounding.

Forecasting

Gradient-Boosted Poisson Model

Features: H3 PICS score, persistence rate, road class, POI proximity (metro station, market), day-of-week seasonality, lag features. Validated with rolling-origin backtesting across 3 folds.

Ranking (3-fold CV): Spearman 0.83
Precision@20: 80% CV / 95% best window
Point MASE: 1.02 (≈ naive) — rank > count
Patrol Route

TSP Heuristic (NN + 2-opt)

Nearest-neighbour initialization followed by 2-opt improvement on haversine distances. Routes the top-N PICS-ranked hotspots. Outputs stop sequence, leg distances, cumulative km, and estimated shift duration.

Route: 34.2 km (12 stops)
Naive: 84.1 km
Saving: 59% shorter

Honest Scope

No live traffic-speed feed exists in the data, so flow impact is grounded in road-network criticality, a measured, defensible proxy. The enforcement-action field is 100% empty, so the impact study leads with the confound-free persistence result and reports the DiD as suggestive only (regression-to-the-mean caveat stated in the platform).

The PICS Formula
PICS = 100 × norm( log1p( severity × persistence × road_factor × junction_factor ) )
Built on the provided BTP dataset + OpenStreetMap (OSMnx) road network, visualised on MapmyIndia (Mappls) interactive maps, partner technology, free tier, fully reproducible.
severity Lane/footpath/junction blockers > generic parking > admin offences
persistence Active days ÷ total span. Chronic spots outrank one-offs.
road_factor OSM road-class weight × lane factor. Arterials carry more flow.
junction_factor Exponential-decay boost within ~40 m of a real intersection.
